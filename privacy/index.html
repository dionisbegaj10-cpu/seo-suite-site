2. Contact form

When you submit the contact form we process the first name, last name, email address, telephone number (optional), the service you selected, and any message you write.

Why

To answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a quote or contract.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given via the checkbox on the form, and — where your enquiry concerns a possible engagement — steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Who receives it

Form submissions are delivered by Web3Forms, acting as a processor on our behalf, and are then held in our email inbox.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months. Where a contract results, statutory retention periods (up to 10 years under German commercial and tax law) apply.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful. Analytics only loads after you accept it in the cookie banner — decline, and no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent.

If you accept, Google Ireland Limited processes usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referring site) and sets cookies to recognise returning visitors. IP anonymisation is enabled. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TTDSG). You can withdraw it at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, with effect for the future.

Retention: Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

4. Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare, Inc. as a content delivery network. Both automatically process connection data — IP address, time of request, page requested, browser and operating system — in server logs, which is technically unavoidable when delivering a website.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure, reliable, and fast website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Cookies

We set one small first-party item in your browser's local storage to remember your cookie choice. It contains only your preference and is strictly necessary, so it needs no consent. All other cookies — the Google Analytics ones — are set only if you accept them.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict how we process it (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out (Art. 7(3))

To exercise any of these, email contact@seosuite.studio. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in Germany, the data protection authority of your federal state.

7. No automated decision-making

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

8. Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and revise the date above.
